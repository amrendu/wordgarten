### Description — exact entered text

```text
Learn German vocabulary from A1 to C1 with illustrated flashcards, pronunciation and practical examples. Build the words you need for everyday life in Germany, your German course and Goethe or telc exam preparation.

Explore over 3,200 German words and expressions by level, topic and exam collection. The app interface is in English. German explanations and example sentences are paired with English meanings and translations.

REMEMBER WORDS WITH FLASHCARDS
Try to recall the meaning before revealing the illustration and answer. Listen to the pronunciation, read the example and choose whether to practise the word again.

FIVE WAYS TO PRACTISE
• Flashcards: recall first, then reveal the meaning and image.
• Review: explore the full card, meaning and example with audio.
• Spelling: write the German word and check your answer.
• Quiz: choose the matching meaning.
• Word forms: practise articles, plurals, verb forms and supported grammar patterns.

VOCABULARY FOR YOUR LEVEL AND GOALS
Choose A1, A2, B1, B2 or C1 and explore themed collections. Use Goethe and telc vocabulary collections to support your exam preparation, alongside your course and other study materials.

LISTEN AND LEARN OFFLINE
Bundled pronunciation recordings play automatically in Flashcards and Review. Replay words and examples whenever you need them. Vocabulary, images and recordings are available offline. Words without a recording use an available system voice.

GROW YOUR WORD GARDEN
Set a daily goal, save favourite words and track your learning progress. Return to words you want to practise and build a regular study habit. Progress stays on your device; no account is required.

START FREE AT EVERY LEVEL
The first general, Goethe and telc category at each level A1–C1 is free, including its practice modes. Premium unlocks the remaining categories and additional tools, including custom word lists and detailed insights.

PREMIUM PURCHASES
Choose a weekly or monthly subscription, or lifetime access with a one-time purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your App Store account settings. After cancellation, access continues until the paid period ends. The App Store shows your local price and purchase confirmation. Use Restore purchases on the paywall with the same store account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription and may remove locally stored learning progress.

Wortgarten is an independent vocabulary-learning app, not an official Goethe-Institut or telc product. It does not guarantee an exam result.

Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Support: support@deutsch-pruefung.de
```
