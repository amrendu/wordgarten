Wortgarten Premium purchases

Apple or Google processes payments for Premium subscriptions and lifetime access. Wortgarten uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, manage subscription entitlements and understand subscription performance.

RevenueCat processes a randomly generated anonymous customer identifier, purchase history and store transaction information, together with relevant app, store, locale and currency information. Wortgarten does not send vocabulary progress, saved words or practice answers to RevenueCat. We do not provide your name, email address or advertising identifier to RevenueCat. Payment card details are handled by your app store and are not received by Wortgarten.

Purchase information is transmitted over encrypted connections and retained to support entitlement verification, restoration, fraud prevention and subscription analytics. It is not used by Wortgarten for advertising tracking. For details about RevenueCat's processing, see RevenueCat's privacy policy.

For privacy questions or purchase-data deletion requests, contact support@deutsch-pruefung.de. We may need purchase information to locate your anonymous record. Deleting the app removes its local learning data but does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel subscriptions through your Apple or Google store settings.
