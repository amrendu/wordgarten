# Age-rating preparation

Completed and saved in App Store Connect. Apple calculated 13+ in 172 countries or regions, with regional exceptions including 12+ in Vietnam and Korea. No override was selected. Earlier operating systems show a global 12+ rating with regional exceptions.

Current app capabilities: no chat, public user content, ads, unrestricted web browser, age assurance or parental controls. Private custom vocabulary lists are stored locally. Practice scores and learning goals do not provide prizes or gambling.

Content audit found neutral vocabulary examples referring to beer, wine and cigarettes. Declare **infrequent alcohol, tobacco or drug references**. These are language-learning examples rather than encouragement to use them.

The vocabulary app provides no medical or treatment advice. The remaining mature-theme, violence and sexuality answers were saved as none.
